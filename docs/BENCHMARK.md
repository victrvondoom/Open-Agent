## Limitations

- Synthetic cases authored by the same team that wrote the policy engine; not an independent test set.
- Sample size is 30 cases; rates are not statistically meaningful beyond this set.
- Model narration ran in deterministic-fallback mode unless model_calls_by_model shows a live model.
- The baseline is deliberately simple and transparent; it is not a competitor product.
- The adversarial cases and the security corpus were written by the same author as the Scout guard; they measure the anticipated attacks, not unseen ones.
- The single-pass-LLM baseline and federated Zetaris queries could not be run (no credentials or egress); they are reported as BLOCKED, not estimated.
